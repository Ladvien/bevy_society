# Changelog

All notable changes to bevy_society. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); the crate is pre-1.0, so any release may break the API.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Startup warnings are now asserted, not read by hand.** `tests/common/capture.rs` is a `LogPlugin` layer that records every event into a resource, so a test can assert a `warn!`. Building it immediately found a defect in the previous iteration's warning: an activity with both a `Field` consideration and a `Gradient` target was counted twice, so one offending definition was reported as `2 definitions … — follow, follow`. Now one. Note the constraint: the `tracing` subscriber is process-global and settable once, so a test binary may build only one capturing app.

- **A startup warning for definitions that rely on unscored field inputs.** `Signal::Field` considerations and `Gradient` targets are accepted by the definitions file with the `fields` feature on, and the decision scores neither — the field grids and `SocialFields::field_value` work, but nothing reads them when choosing. `BehaviorPlugin` now warns and names the offending activities, and `SocietyDefs::{activity_signals,has_gradient_activity}` plus a public `Signal` let a host run the same check. Why the wiring could not be completed this iteration is written up in `docs/loop/needs-decision/field-inputs-are-unscored.md`.

- **A cost harness, and the numbers behind the scale claim.** `examples/benchmark` reports milliseconds per society tick for a given population. Measured on an M1 Max, release, starter pack: 0.55 ms per tick at 100 persons, **14.1 ms at 1 000** (4.9% of a 60 fps frame at `tick_secs = 0.25`), and **351.6 ms at 5 000** (140% of a frame — unplayable). Reproducible to 0.01%, and quadratic exactly as `docs/guide/concepts.md` claimed: 25× the pairs costs 25.0× the time. The guide's Scale section now carries the table instead of the word "fine".

- **`ctx.tellable(a)`**, and a starter `gossip` that uses it. `ctx.knows` needs a rumor id, which an authored definitions file has no way to name, so no script could ask "does this person have anything to tell?". `ctx.tellable(a)` answers whether a person knows a rumor they are still spreading, and the starter pack's `gossip` now scores 0 without one — gossip carries reputation information (Sommerfeld et al. 2007), so a villager with none converses instead. Measured in the village: `chat` 8746 → 9312 person-ticks, `gossip` down 6%, and `snub` down **73%** (6004 → 1617), because fewer gratuitous gossips means fewer insults and fewer grudges.

- **A startup warning for degenerate activity durations.** `ActivitySpec::duration_hours` is game hours while a tick is `dt_days`, so a short `seconds_per_day` can make an activity last a single tick — at which point a person re-decides every tick and repeats whatever scored highest. That was silent, and cost three iterations of findings. `BehaviorPlugin` now warns with the activity's own arithmetic (`snub (0.1h = 1.1 ticks)`), and `SocietyDefs::activity_durations()` lets a host make the same check.

- **The hostility runaway was three things, and none was missing damping.** `snub`'s grudge curve saturated at grudge 0.4, so one witnessed insult made snubbing the best option forever: `Linear(2.5, 0)` becomes `Linear(1.0, 0)`. The cluster scenario gave its people no places, so gossip held the social need high enough that `chat`'s own `Need(social) Linear(-1, 1)` gate never opened — measured, `chat` ran zero times in 2000 ticks — and each group now has a hearth. And the evidence harness ran a 60× shorter game-day than the definitions were calibrated for, where a 0.1-hour `snub` lasted 1.07 ticks and people re-decided every tick. See `docs/research/rumor-and-reputation.md` (Takács, Flache & Mäs 2016, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0157948, for why no damping rule was added).

- **Rivalry, and three defects that stood in its way.** `snub` was gated on *low* affinity, so a village snubbed strangers and never anyone it knew; it now reads `ctx.grudge(actor, target)`. Script considerations could not see their target at all (`target` was bound to −1), so no `ctx` call could score a `Person` option. And `witness_reaction` signed approval by the witness's opinion of the *norm* rather than of the *act*, so a kindness-valuing witness rewarded an insult: it now uses `norm · sign(harm)`. Measured, `rumor-crosses-a-cluster` goes from 11 rivals to 30 and `need-cycle` from 0 to 21. See `docs/research/rumor-and-reputation.md` (Bondü & Richter 2016, DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00795, for the mechanism).

- **`chat` and `gossip` satisfy `social` while they run.** They were the two activities that serve the social need and the only two that declared no `satisfies` rate, so time spent talking counted for nothing while `eat` (+1.2/h) and `sleep` (+0.15/h) both paid out. Measured over 4200 ticks at the same seed, mean `social` rose from 0.105 to 0.225.

- **Thought barks on activity start.** An activity's `barks` lines are written as `SocietyBark { thought: true }` when it starts, with `bark_chance`, through a system that reads `ActivityStarted` so the draw happens once per start in `PersonIndex` order. The dead-code inventory is now empty: every public item the crate declares is reachable.

- **Movement and gossip reach the capture.** `Destination` was set only for a `Point` target, so a village whose activities all target places never walked: everybody stood where they spawned. Every target now gets one — a place's transform, or a person's position offset by `0.75 × interaction_radius` — and `tests/behavior.rs` pins both. The starter pack's `gossip` activity is gated on distance rather than familiarity, so a rumor can spread in one evening instead of waiting for ties, and the capture counts barks across its settle steps rather than only the recorded frames.

- **Daily life and social exchanges (`BehaviorPlugin`).** Autonomous people choose activities by compensated utility over needs, mood, personality, emotions, ties and the clock; they travel to a point, place, home or person, arrive within `interaction_radius`, satisfy needs at the definition's per-hour rate, and end an activity on duration, on a lost person target, or on a travel timeout. A social exchange runs the `requires` script, the acceptance draw, both effect sets, and on acceptance opinion influence, gossip, a contest, a judged deed, triadic closure and intergroup contact; both sides record an `Interaction` memory and gain exposure. `InteractionResolved`, `ActivityStarted` and `ActivityEnded` report it, and the trigger queue sees exchanges and activities in the same tick. See `docs/research/needs-and-utility.md` (Mark & Dill, Game AI Pro 3 ch. 13; Dill 2015, DOI 10.1201/b18373-5).

- **Examples scaffolding.** `examples/common/recorder.rs` is the headless recorder the capture example drives: no window, no winit, synchronous pipeline compilation, an off-screen render target, a hand-pumped loop with a device poll per frame, and `TimeUpdateStrategy::ManualDuration(1/30 s)` so a captured frame is exactly one thirtieth of a second of game time. `tools/gif.sh` is the other half: a two-pass palette encode with the caption burned in.

- **Script runtime and rules (`RulesPlugin`).** The `ctx` / `fx` Rhai API over a per-tick snapshot: `ctx` reads the society as it stood at the tick start and `fx` queues writes that one exclusive system applies after `SocietySystems::Rules`. Triggers fire on `Tick`, `Hour`, `Custom` (`FireTrigger`), `TieLabelChanged`, `NormViolated`, `NeedBelow` and `RumorReach`, honouring `once` and `cooldown_hours`; a failed condition counts as false and a failed effect applies none of its actions. Every evaluation is sandboxed (10 000 operations, bounded strings, arrays and maps).

- **Dialogue (`DialoguePlugin`, feature `dialogue`).** Conversations and barks through `bevy_speech_bubbles` at its pinned rev. `TalkTo { npc, player }` opens the highest-priority conversation rule whose `if` holds against the tick-start snapshot, and is ignored while a conversation is open. Node effects run their script; each finished conversation records a `Talked` memory for both people and a two-way exposure. `fx.converse` queues a `TalkTo`. `SocietyBark` lines are forwarded under the speaker key `society:<PersonId>`, which lives as long as the person. The plugin runs in `Update` because speech bubbles are frame-driven.

- **Beliefs, rumors and reputation (`BeliefsPlugin`).** Opinions per topic with bounded-confidence influence, Friedkin–Johnsen anchor pull and conformity to a strong group mean; a rumor book with the Daley–Kendall stifling rule and trust-scaled belief; and the witness, patient and actor reactions to a deed, including `NormViolated`. `PlantRumor` starts a rumor from the game or a script. See `docs/research/opinion-dynamics.md` and `docs/research/rumor-and-reputation.md` (Deffuant et al. 2000, DOI 10.1142/S0219525900000078; Daley & Kendall 1964, DOI 10.1038/2041118a0; Ohtsuki & Iwasa 2004, DOI 10.1016/j.jtbi.2004.06.005).

- **Engine-free model math (`bevy_society::model`).** Response curves, the Mehrabian/ALMA personality-to-mood baseline, power-law familiarity and exposure, Deffuant opinion influence with anchor pull and conformity, Daley–Kendall stifling, and triadic balance. Each formula's DOI sits in a comment beside it.

- **Relationships (`RelationshipsPlugin`).** Directed, weighted ties per person with power-law familiarity (Anderson & Schooler 1991), mere exposure (Zajonc 1968), affinity relaxation toward zero, Heider structural balance over sampled triads, a Dunbar-style cap on ties, and labels (stranger, acquaintance, friend, rival) with a `TieLabelChanged` message. See `docs/research/relationships.md` (Heider 1946, DOI 10.1080/00223980.1946.9917275; Dunbar 1992, DOI 10.1016/0047-2484(92)90081-J).

- **Groups and status (`GroupsPlugin`).** Faction memberships with an in-group bias, standing as respect conferred by familiar persons, logistic contests weighted by dominance and standing, intergroup contact (Pettigrew & Tropp 2006), and per-faction cohesion and regard each tick. See `docs/research/groups-and-status.md` (Tajfel et al. 1971, DOI 10.1002/ejsp.2420010202; Bonabeau et al. 1995, DOI 10.1016/0378-4371(95)00064-e).

- **Social fields (`fields` feature).** Per-cell crowd presence and commotion grids plus one presence grid per faction, deposited in person order and evaporated per game-day on `bevy_stigmergy`. Schelling's tolerance input is the `OwnShare` field (own / (own + other)), and `Gradient` activity targets follow the field toward or away from a cluster. See `docs/research/groups-and-status.md` (Schelling 1971, DOI 10.1080/0022250X.1971.9989794).

- **Mind (`MindPlugin`).** Emotions (OCC kinds with ALMA PAD values) are appraised by `Appraise` messages and decay with a 0.1 game-day half-life. Mood is pulled toward the intensity-weighted emotion centre and returns to a personality baseline (Mehrabian 1996; Gebhard 2005), with Hatfield-style contagion from affinity-weighted contacts. Needs decay at `decay_per_day`. See `docs/research/personality-and-mood.md` (DOI 10.1080/00049539608259510).
