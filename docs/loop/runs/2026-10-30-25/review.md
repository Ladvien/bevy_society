# Review: `Tuning::forgiveness` 12.0

Evidence: `evidence/plots/before-after.png` (rivals and friends over time, four runs), `evidence/plots/sweep.png` (gain sweep), `evidence/{before,after}/*/summary.json`, `narrative.txt`, `grudge_sources.txt`, `eval.md`.

## 1. Behavior: GREAT

**Critique.** The papers say forgiveness follows relationship value and low exploitation risk, carried by trust; they give no rate in game time, so 12.0 (a 20-hour half-life toward someone trusted at 0.5, 63 hours toward a serial offender at 0.1) is this crate's compression, not theirs, and McCullough et al.'s logarithmic shape is not modelled: only the rate per relationship is. A first try at gains 1-4 did nearly nothing in the village (`eval.md`, sweep table): trust in a feuding pair falls with each insult, so forgiveness is weakest exactly where the ping-pong is, which is the model working as the papers describe, and it takes a gain near 10 for the trusted majority of ties to matter.

**Against the scenario.** `tests/forgiveness.rs`: three days after the same insult, the grievance toward a trusted offender is under a trigger's 0.35 bar and toward a distrusted one is over it; off, both are over. In the society, every one of six runs moves the same way (`eval.md`): insults fall 13-37%, compliments to insults go from 2.8-3.3:1 to 3.9-5.2:1 (Baumeister et al. 2001's floor is 5:1), directed rival ties fall 32-69% and friend ties rise 18-117%. Snubs answering an insult received fall from 56.9% to 50.9% of all snubs (village s7, `grudge_sources.txt`). Bram's day (`after/v7/narrative.txt`): his friend Garet, after five days of compliments both ways, insults him on day 9; Bram answers that evening, Garet once more the next day (refused), Bram once more on day 15 (refused), and the compliments stop. Before, Bram and Corin traded ten insults.

## 2. Zoom: GREAT

**Critique.** `before-after.png`: after the change rivals **plateau** in the last third of every run (village 24→24, 19→18, 26→28; town 123→111) where HEAD still climbs (town 223→277 over the last ten days). Nothing clips: rival ties stay above zero in every run (18-36 in the village), and friends do not saturate (47-65 of the village's 552 ordered pairs, 9-12%; 86-101 of the town's 3 540, 2-3%). Mean pleasure in the village goes from slightly negative (-0.030 to -0.041) to slightly positive (+0.007 to +0.025); the town stays negative (-0.08 to -0.09) because its third need starves (mean 0.03-0.04 before and after, pre-existing). Need means are unchanged to two decimals. One feature looked like a spike and is not: village s7 loses nine friend ties between day 15.10 and 15.28 (zoomed from `series.csv`, a gradual walk with the usual ±1 label flicker at the friend band), the same size as HEAD's own dips (s42 HEAD falls 47→30 over days 10-12). The sweep is smooth in the town (277/205/148/111/89/53 rival ties at 0/4/8/12/16/32): no cliff, unlike the `snub` exponent sweep of pass 24 (140/49/0). Residual: the fresh town seed 99 still holds more rival ties than friend ties (155 to 86).

## 3. Fresh eyes: GREAT

**Critique.** Seed 99 was not used to choose the gain. Village s99: rivals 66→36, friends 35→54, insults 361→249; town s99: rivals 229→155, friends 52→86, insults 1720→1491, the same direction as the tuned seeds and a smaller step in the town. Off is HEAD: `--forgiveness 0` gives a byte-identical `summary.json` and narrative at village s7, so no host that sets it loses anything. To a host developer the change is one paragraph (`docs/guide/concepts.md`): "a grudge fades faster toward someone you still trust; a person who keeps offending loses your trust and stays resented", with one knob and its off value. The full release suite (94 binaries) is green at 12.0, including the chaotic-equality pins in `label_hysteresis` and the rumor book, which 16.0 failed by 0.1 point.
