Already the basis of `Tuning::negativity_ratio` (note: `docs/research/rumor-and-reputation.md`). Bondü & Richter 2016, DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00795: snubbing is reactive to provocation (a grudge), correlational, so the curve shape is a design default.
