# Research — a refractory period for harmful retaliation

Source: Gouldner 1960, "The Norm of Reciprocity", DOI 10.2307/2092623: a harm is answered in kind (negative reciprocity) and the debt is then discharged. The crate answered a harm repeatedly: `snub` scores on `ctx.grudge`, which outlives the insult it answers (`docs/loop/runs/2026-10-28-10/eval.md`: 73% of snubs at 24 people, 55% at 60, answer an insult the snubber received). The paper fixes no interval, so `retaliation_refractory_hours` is a design default, opt-in (`None`), like `failed_place_memory_hours`.
