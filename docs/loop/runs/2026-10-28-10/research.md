# 2026-10-28-10 — research

No new model: a measurement of the existing one. Mechanism under test is Bondü & Richter 2016 (DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00795), reactive aggression as a reaction to how one has been treated, which is what `snub`'s only consideration (`ctx.grudge`) encodes. Retaliation as a reciprocal spiral is the claim; Gollwitzer, Meder & Schmitt 2011 (DOI 10.1002/ejsp.782) was the previous pass's (falsified) attempt to stop it by settling the actor's side. Believable output: a quarrel that escalates and then ends, not a standing ping-pong.
