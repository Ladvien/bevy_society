# Research — a crowded place counts whoever is at it

Issue #25 (Sanctuary). No new paper: the allowance is the crate's existing design default, `interaction_radius + crowded_arrival_growth · sqrt(crowd)` (square root because a crowd occupies *area*; one body diameter per `sqrt(n)`; run `2026-12-24-01`). The defect is what `crowd` counts: bodies inside the fixed `interaction_radius` disc, while the allowance admits people *outside* it, so admitted people stand where the count cannot see them and the allowance freezes. The fix is a counting rule, not a new model: `crowd` = max(bodies inside the disc, people whose `Doing` targets the place with `arrived_day` set). Believable output: a place's seated crowd keeps admitting the queue behind it up to `radius + growth·sqrt(n)`, interrupted `sleep`s fall, completed endings do not.
